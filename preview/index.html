Groaning for Glory

Text

Romans 8:28 — And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.

Exegetical Proposition

Paul does not promise that all things are good, but that God works all things together for good for those who are called according to His purpose.

Big Idea

God's purpose holds every groan of the saints.

Outline

  • The certainty of the promise — "we know"
  • The scope of the promise — "all things"
  • The recipients of the promise — "them that love God"
  • The ground of the promise — "his purpose"

Body

The word συνεργεῖ (G4903) carries the sense of a co-working, a weaving together. Paul is not a fatalist; he is a weaver. The shuttle of providence passes over threads of joy and threads of grief alike, and the pattern is only visible from the finished side.

Application

Name the thread you are holding today. You cannot see the pattern. But the Weaver can.
